The Fine Print, Plainly

Your data. Your device. Your call.

Money data is sensitive. TrueSummit is built so you're never guessing where yours goes.

  • AI insights stay on-device Apple only

    Apple Intelligence generates them locally — your transactions aren't sent out to produce a trend or a nudge. On Android, TrueSummit runs without this feature.

  • Export or erase everything

    Settings → Privacy & Data shows exactly what syncs, and lets you export or delete it all in one place.

  • Bank logins never touch our servers

    Account connections run through Plaid — your bank credentials go to Plaid and your bank, not to TrueSummit.
